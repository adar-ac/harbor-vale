# Model Card for Random Forest Model

## Purpose
The purpose of this model is to predict customer churn for a telecommunications company. By identifying customers who are likely to churn, the company can take proactive measures to retain them.

## Model Details
- **Model Type:** Random Forest
- **Selected Based On:** Highest mean cross-validated ROC-AUC score
- **Parameters:** 
  - Class Weight: Balanced
  - Number of Estimators: 300
  - Minimum Samples Leaf: 5
  - Random State: 42

## Training Data Summary
- **Source Files:** clean.csv, dataset_contract.json
- **Contract Version:** 1.0
- **Rows:** 7032
- **Target Variable:** Churn
- **Class Counts:** 
  - No: 5163
  - Yes: 1869
- **Churn Rate (%):** 26.58
- **Dropped Identifier Columns:** customerID
- **Numeric Features Used:** tenure, MonthlyCharges, TotalCharges
- **Categorical Features Encoded:** gender, SeniorCitizen, Partner, Dependents, PhoneService, MultipleLines, InternetService, OnlineSecurity, OnlineBackup, DeviceProtection, TechSupport, StreamingTV, StreamingMovies, Contract, PaperlessBilling, PaymentMethod
- **Number of Model Features After Encoding:** 30

## Limitations
1. **Static Snapshot:** The model is trained on a static snapshot of data, which may not capture temporal changes in customer behavior or market conditions.
2. **Fixed Threshold:** The decision threshold is fixed at 0.5, which may not be optimal for all business scenarios. Adjustments may be necessary based on specific business needs.
3. **Tenure-0 Customers Removed:** Customers with zero tenure were removed during data cleaning, which may introduce bias as these customers could have different churn behaviors.
4. **Correlated Features:** Some features may be correlated, which could affect the interpretability of the model and lead to multicollinearity issues.

## Ethical Considerations
1. **Sensitive Attributes:** The model includes sensitive attributes such as gender and SeniorCitizen status. Care should be taken to ensure that these features do not lead to biased or discriminatory outcomes.
2. **Use of Scores:** The scores generated by the model should be used to inform business decisions and not as the sole basis for determining customer treatment. It is essential to consider the broader context and individual circumstances of customers when interpreting the results.
