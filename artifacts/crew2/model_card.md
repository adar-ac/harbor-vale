# Model Card for Random Forest Model

## Purpose
The purpose of this model is to predict customer churn for a telecommunications company. By identifying customers who are likely to leave, the company can take proactive measures to retain them.

## Model Details
The selected model is a Random Forest classifier. It was chosen based on its performance, achieving a mean cross-validated ROC-AUC of 0.8468, which is 0.0008 higher than the runner-up model, Logistic Regression, which had a mean CV ROC-AUC of 0.8460. Given that the selection margin is small and the models are effectively equal on the selection metric, the choice of Random Forest could also be justified by its ability to capture complex interactions between features, although Logistic Regression offers easier interpretability through coefficients.

## Limitations
1. **Static Snapshot**: The model is trained on a static snapshot of data, which may not capture changes in customer behavior over time.
2. **Fixed 0.5 Threshold**: The decision threshold for classifying churn is fixed at 0.5, which may not be optimal for all business scenarios. Adjusting this threshold could yield different results.
3. **Tenure-0 Customers Removed**: Customers with zero tenure were removed during data cleaning, which may introduce bias if these customers have different churn characteristics.
4. **Correlated Features**: Some features may be correlated, which can affect the model's interpretability and performance.

## Ethical Considerations
The model includes sensitive attributes such as gender and SeniorCitizen status. Care should be taken to ensure that the model's predictions do not reinforce biases or lead to discriminatory practices. Additionally, the scores generated by the model should not be used as the sole basis for decision-making regarding customer treatment; they should be considered alongside other qualitative factors to ensure fair and equitable outcomes.
