Overview

Data Cleaning Process

The cleaning process involved dropping 11 rows due to blank values in the TotalCharges column. These rows corresponded to customers with a tenure of 0, indicating they were never billed.

After cleaning, the dataset was saved in clean.csv.

Key Observations

  • The churn rate is significantly higher among senior citizens.
  • Customers with a month-to-month contract have the highest churn rate.
  • Churn rates are lower for customers with partners and dependents.
  • Online security and backup services correlate with lower churn rates.
